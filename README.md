# A3 – Superhot VR (Assessment 3)

Meta Quest 2 VR game (Unity 6, XR Interaction Toolkit). Extends the A2 prototype: [A2-Superhot](https://github.com/umed-khasanov/A2-Superhot).

## Concept
Stylised low-poly "earth magic" look: the ruins of a city floating in the sky,
attacked by one-eyed drones. The player fights with fists and thrown objects
(A2 hit and throw system) across two levels.

## Status
Work in progress.
- Done: art pipeline, demo city scene (`Assets/A3/Scenes/A3_DemoCity.unity`)
- Planned: flying drone enemy with health, waves/two levels, player damage feedback, thrown props

## Art and credits
All assets are CC0 and kept light for Quest 2:
- Kenney Fantasy Town Kit (kenney.nl) – ruins, walls, props (single colour-palette texture)
- Quaternius Sci-Fi Essentials Kit, free version (quaternius.com) – one-eyed drone enemies.
  Enemy textures downscaled to 1024 px; normal and ORM maps not used.

The demo city is generated by an editor script: `Tools > A3 > Build Demo City`.
Ground is one flat disc and the city is marked Batching Static to keep draw calls low.

## Requirements
Unity 6000.3.22f1, XR Interaction Toolkit 3.3.2, XR Hands 1.9.0, OpenXR, Meta Quest 2.

## How to run
Open this folder in Unity Hub, open the scene in `Assets/A3/Scenes/`, press Play (Meta XR Simulator) or Build And Run to Quest 2.
